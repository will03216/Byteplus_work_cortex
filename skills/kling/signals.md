## Signal Engagement Hierarchy
- POST (they published content about AI video, cinematic production, or character animation) → HIGH signal
- COMMENT (they engaged in depth with relevant content) → MEDIUM signal
- LIKE / REACTION → MEDIUM-LOW signal (still actionable for right ICP)

A like from a Creative Director, VFX Supervisor, or Brand Director is medium-low, not low.
A like on Kling-specific content (realism demos, character fidelity examples, image-to-video showcases, cinematic quality comparisons) from a strong-fit company/persona is stronger than a generic like.
Only assign "low" when BOTH are true: wrong ICP segment AND wrong persona.

## Kling-Specific Buying Signals to Flag
These indicate a buyer evaluating or ready to adopt AI video for quality-first use cases:

High-intent signals:
- Posts or comments about character consistency, visual realism, or cinematic quality in AI video
- Comparing AI video tools for a premium production project
- Exploring image-to-video for fashion/luxury/beauty content
- VFX supervisor or DP asking about AI tools for scene generation

Medium-intent signals:
- Following or commenting on AI video generation demos
- Posts about AI adoption in production workflow
- Hiring for AI-integrated creative roles
- Attending or presenting at digital production or VFX conferences

Company momentum signals (flag in observed_evidence):
- Fashion or luxury brand expanding into video content
- Production company adding AI/technology department
- New Creative Director or Head of Production recently hired (< 12 months)
- Recent brand campaign requiring high-volume high-quality video

## New Executive Signal (High Priority)
If the person recently started their role (< 12 months), flag this explicitly in observed_evidence.
New creative leaders are actively evaluating tools and have budget discretion to make changes.
