## LinkedIn Signal Taxonomy

Signal types ranked by intent strength (highest to lowest):
1. linkedin_post — Person published original content → HIGH intent signal
2. linkedin_comment — Person commented in depth → MEDIUM intent signal
3. linkedin_engagement / linkedin_reaction / linkedin_like — Person liked or reacted → MEDIUM-LOW signal

A like from the right persona in the right vertical is NOT a disqualifying signal.
Assign "low" signal_strength only when BOTH of these are true:
- Wrong ICP segment (company has no plausible use case)
- Wrong persona (junior role or irrelevant function)

Signal strength values: "high" | "medium" | "medium-low" | "low"
